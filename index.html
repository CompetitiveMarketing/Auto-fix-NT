Do you service utes and 4WDs?

Yes. Auto Fix NT provides servicing for cars, utes and 4WDs. Let the workshop know your make, model and what you need when you enquire.

Can I book a registration inspection?

Yes. Contact the workshop to discuss your vehicle type, the inspection required and available times. An enquiry is not a confirmed appointment.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Your contact details, vehicle make and model, and a description of the issue. For servicing, include the odometer reading if you know it.

Where is the workshop?

On the Stuart Highway in Alice Springs, opposite Bunnings. Use the directions link below or call the team before heading in.

Is my booking confirmed when I send an enquiry?

No. The workshop needs to confirm the appointment with you. If the matter is urgent, please call 0400 054 502.
